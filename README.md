# NavPlayer

Music player for your car head unit. Connect to your [Navidrome](https://www.navidrome.org/) server and play your library from the dash.

## What you can do

- **Random** — shuffle and play your music
- **Playlists** — tap a playlist to play it
- **Player bar** — seek, skip tracks, play and pause; tap the song name for more
- Keeps playing when you switch to another app

## First-time setup

1. Install NavPlayer on your head unit.
2. Open **Settings** (gear icon).
3. Enter your server address, username, and password.
4. Tap **Test connection**, then **Save**.

Your server address is usually something like `https://music.example.com:4533` — use the same URL you use in a browser or the Navidrome app.

## Head unit options

In **Settings → Head unit**:

- **Start on boot** — open NavPlayer when the car starts (on by default)
- **Driving mode** — bigger buttons and a simpler layout; **long-press Playlists** to open settings again

The screen stays on while music is playing. Steering-wheel and Bluetooth play/skip buttons work as usual.
