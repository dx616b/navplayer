# NavPlayer

Music player for your car. Install it on your Android head unit, connect to your [Navidrome](https://www.navidrome.org/) server, and play your library from the dash.

## In the car

- **Random** — shuffle and play your music
- **Playlists** — tap a playlist to play it
- **Player bar** — seek, skip, play and pause; tap the song name for more
- **Driving mode** — large buttons and a simple layout for use while driving
- Steering-wheel and Bluetooth skip/play buttons work as usual
- Screen stays on while music is playing

## Setup (do this while parked)

1. Install NavPlayer on your head unit.
2. Open **Settings** (gear icon).
3. Enter your server address, username, and password.
4. Tap **Test connection**, then **Save**.

Your server address is whatever runs your Navidrome music — for example `https://music.example.com`. The car needs internet to reach it (phone hotspot or the head unit’s own data).

If you are not sure what to enter, ask whoever set up your music server.

## Options

In **Settings → Head unit**:

- **Start on boot** — open NavPlayer when you start the car (on by default)
- **Driving mode** — recommended for daily driving; **long-press Playlists** to open settings again
